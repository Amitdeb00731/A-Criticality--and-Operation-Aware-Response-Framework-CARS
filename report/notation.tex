\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent
S7 addressing follows the Siemens convention used throughout: \texttt{\%I} for process inputs and \texttt{\%Q} for process outputs at the bit level, \texttt{\%ID} and \texttt{\%QD} for the corresponding double-word (32-bit) analogue channels, and \texttt{\%M} for internal memory bits (merkers). For example, \texttt{\%ID100} is the analogue level input from the tank sensor, \texttt{\%QD100} and \texttt{\%QD104} drive the fill and discharge valves, and \texttt{\%M0.1} and \texttt{\%M0.2} are the HMI start and stop bits.
